## Post-release scrolling hotfix

### Symptom

After opening the production APK, the Profile dashboard could remain unable to scroll after the startup and release-notes experiences closed.

### Cause

The private workspace loader and release-notes dialog managed `document.body` scrolling independently. On a fresh launch they could overlap and release in the opposite order, restoring `overflow: hidden` after the last overlay closed.

### Resolution

The startup experience and modal system now share a reference-counted body-scroll lock. Scrolling is restored only after the final lock holder releases, while modal-only root accessibility behavior remains intact. A regression test reproduces the startup-first release order that caused the installed-app failure.

Because `/app` and other authenticated paths bypass offline navigation caches, fully closing and reopening an installed APK fetches the corrected production shell. Users do not need to reinstall or download another APK for this web-only fix.
