- Status: live
- Release date: 2026-08-12
- Production site: <https://zoption.site>
- Production API: <https://api.zoption.site>

Zoption 2.1.0 makes the new Financial Assistant voice experience the headline feature. Product-facing changes are summarized in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Voice implementation

- Voice recording stops automatically after speech is followed by silence, while no-speech recordings end locally without an empty transcription request.
- Review-first remains the default. A user can instead choose automatic submission after transcription finishes.
- Reply preferences support spoken-and-text or text-only answers and are saved per user.
- Spoken replies expose a preparation state and remove markdown, tables, and raw URLs before synthesis for more natural playback.
- Recording, audio graph, stream, timer, and playback resources are released when recording ends or the assistant route closes.
- Voice mode remains separately consented and authenticated. Anonymous requests to the live transcription route are rejected.
