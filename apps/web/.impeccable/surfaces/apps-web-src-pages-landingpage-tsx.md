# Landing Page Surface Brief

## Scope and Visitor Mode
Public marketing and conversion gateway for Zoption. Mode is **Persuade**.

## Audience & Job
Individual budgeters who want a calm, private, offline-first personal financial workspace without linking bank credentials.

## Chosen Direction & Memorable Moment
- **Direction**: Artisanal, editorial financial clarity with Newsreader display serif, crisp Manrope interface typography, tactile paper canvas, and rich emerald/forest tones.
- **Signature Experience**: Interactive hero preview dashboard with real-time 6-month spending rhythm inspection, live category budget gauges, dynamic compounding interest calculator, and transparent fee-aware transfer demonstration.

## Constraints & Principles
- 100% test compatibility with all navigation links, theme toggle, and module sections.
- Zero layout thrashing, zero unnecessary dependencies.
- Full parity across Light, Dark, and Coffee color modes.
